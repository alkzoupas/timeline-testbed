## What it shows

- **Confirmed as pre-registered.** On worlds the pilot never touched, Haiku few-shot ranks pair 3
  at 0.76. Both F64 models are at chance (0.50–0.51), and both gaps exclude 0 by more than 0.12.
  Each world clears 0.5 on its own (lower bounds 0.70 and 0.60).
- **Smaller than M8, as the prereg expected.** 0.84 was the best of eight cells (winner's curse);
  0.76 sits inside M8's interval [0.74, 0.92]. The prereg's power was about 0.92 at a true 0.75.
- **The few-shot examples matter.** Zero-shot is 0.58 pooled and is inconsistent across worlds
  (0.47 and 0.68), with neither gap over F64 excluding 0. The examples come from other mechanisms
  (pairs 4–6), so the gain isn't pair 3 leaking in. Why they help isn't tested; one guess is that
  they set how strongly Haiku should weigh timeline shape.
- **The control passes but is weak evidence.** At 40 + 40 units it catches only a large artifact
  (stated in the prereg). Its point is 0.57, and F64 trees reaches 0.61 on the same world, so the
  *s* = 0 world carries some chance structure that both pick up. The primary result is 0.19 above
  the control.
- **Draws are noisier on pair 3 alone** than across M8's four families (Pearson 0.40 against
  0.59). One likely reason is the narrower range of scores within one family. The mean of three
  draws carries the result, and draw 1 alone still clears 0.5.

## What it doesn't show

It covers one family, at *s* = 0.75, on synthetic worlds. The model is a hosted one, not the small
local LLM of SPEC §2's claim; the probe's held-out null (G2) stands. Nothing here says which
render features carry the signal. The `runs/confirm-*` worlds stay unopened. Per the prereg,
`reports/RESULT.md` changes its headline to "null, except one timeline-only mechanism a hosted
model reads without training on it". Whether to follow it up (for example, which render features
carry it) is the owner's call.
