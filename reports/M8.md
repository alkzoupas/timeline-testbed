## What it shows

- **NO-GO on the pre-registered bar.** The points are 0.56 and 0.55, the bounds 0.487 and 0.479,
  and neither variant beats F64 trees (both difference intervals contain 0). The gaps over F64
  logistic exclude 0, but logistic is below chance on these units, so they show no skill.
- **One family stands out: pair 3** (template operator against office card). Few-shot ranks it at
  0.84 and zero-shot at 0.68, where F64 trees gets 0.56. The few-shot examples for side B came from
  pairs 4–6, so this is held-out. Pair 3 is also the family M4's probe saw in-family (0.94; oracle
  0.99). It is a post-hoc subgroup, one of eight pair × variant cells, on a G2 gate world, and
  without the *s* = 0 control a render artifact can't be ruled out. Reported, not claimed.
- Pairs 2, 4 and 6 sit near chance for both variants (0.46–0.61; every interval contains 0.5).
- **Noise isn't the reason.** Reliability is far higher than the smoke's 0.22 on 10 units: Haiku's
  ranking is stable, but outside pair 3 it doesn't follow the class. Draw 1 alone (zero-shot 0.583,
  95% interval above 0.5) is reported, not gating; the prereg rules out using it as a rescue.
- Following up pair 3 would be a pivot: a DECISIONS note first, then a new pre-registration on
  worlds the pilot didn't touch, with the *s* = 0 control. That's the owner's call.
