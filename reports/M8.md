# M8: Claude Haiku 5.5 pilot (in progress, 2026-10-22)

The question (DECISIONS 2026-10-22, pivot): can Claude Haiku 5.5, zero-shot and with held-out
few-shot, rank timeline-only abuse above its legitimate twins on families it wasn't shown? It's a
hosted reference point, not SPEC §2's small local model, and it can't rescue G2. The plan is in
`context/m8-haiku-kickoff.md`.

## Feasibility smoke (step 3), 2026-10-22

Route: headless Claude Code 2.1.308 on the owner's subscription (Pro), `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=
~/.claude-haiku-scorer`, `--safe-mode --tools "" --strict-mcp-config --no-session-persistence`, a
custom system prompt, `--json-schema` (integer 0–100), an explicit environment allowlist, and an
empty, neutral temp directory as cwd. The prompt is a draft of `m8-v1`, not frozen (system sha256
`0f6af5ef…`). 50 calls on tuning-world renders (`runs/m4-tune-s0.75-seed301`, units drawn with seed
20261022); no labels were read. Scratch scripts and raw logs are in `runs/m8-smoke/`.

| Wave | Calls | OK / parsed | Wall s, p50 (max) | Input tok, mean | Output tok, mean (thinking) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| zero-shot | 30 | 30 / 30 | 11.0 (24.1) | 2,214 | 2,467 (2,414) |
| repeats, 2 waves of 5 at once | 10 | 10 / 10 | 10.2 (25.3) | 2,243 | 2,594 (2,541) |
| `--effort low` | 5 | 5 / 5 | 9.6 (11.6) | 2,188 | 1,616 (1,563) |
| few-shot size (placeholder verdicts) | 2 | 2 / 2 | 6.3 (7.1) | 6,243 | 923 (870) |
| isolation probes | 3 | 3 / – | 6.2–15.7 | 882–5,362 | 1,000–2,814 |

- **Isolation.** Haiku sees only our system prompt. It has no tools apart from Claude Code's
  StructuredOutput tool, which appears only when a schema is passed, and no memory, files or
  CLAUDE.md. The scorer wrote nothing to `~/.claude/projects`, the handoffs or the Hindsight spool;
  the cwd stayed empty and the repo untouched. Claude Code adds an environment block (cwd,
  platform, OS, date, model, a note on untrusted data) and a note with the account identifier, and
  no flag removes them. The first cwd named the project, so the calls moved to a neutral temp
  directory (owner, DECISIONS).
- **Route.** Every call reported `claude-haiku-5-5`; every scored call took 2 turns (the
  StructuredOutput call).
  There were no API errors or limit messages. Five calls at once worked, at about 5 s per call
  against 11 s one at a time. Thinking is on by default and is about 98% of the output;
  `--effort low` cuts it by about 35% but doesn't turn it off. Notional list cost was $0.07 for 50
  calls (`total_cost_usd`; a subscription isn't billed per call, and the Pro limits aren't visible).
- **Scores.** 30 zero-shot units gave 15 distinct values, clustered (72 five times, 88 six times).
- **Repeatability is poor.** Re-scoring 10 units gives Pearson 0.22 and Spearman 0.25, with a mean
  |Δ| of 29 (max 68); 5 of 10 land on the same side of 50. The spread within a unit (SD 25.4) is
  nearly the whole spread (SD 27.5). Low effort against the default gives Pearson 0.08 (5 units).
  A single draw is mostly noise, and Claude Code can't set the temperature.

## Pilot sizing (the owner's decision)

Planning arithmetic, not a result. It takes a draw reliability of 0.2 (from 10 units, so the
interval is wide), averages *k* draws per unit (Spearman–Brown) and assumes binormal scores with
80 + 80 units per side. The go-bar's 95% half-width is about 0.06.

| True AUC | *k* = 1 (640 calls, ~1 h) | *k* = 3 (1,920, ~3 h) | *k* = 5 (3,200, ~4.5 h) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.65: expected observed AUC, power | 0.57, 0.57 | 0.60, 0.88 | 0.61, 0.95 |
| 0.75: expected observed AUC, power | 0.62, 0.97 | 0.67, 1.00 | 0.69, 1.00 |

Hours assume 5 calls at once. The driver must stop cleanly on a limit error and resume.
