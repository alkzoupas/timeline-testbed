pairs 4–6, rank pair 3 abusive above its legitimate twins, and above both held-out F64 models?**

## Scoring (once, after every draw is done)

- **Draws:** M8's rules unchanged (two retries of an unparsed draw; the unit's score is the mean
  of its parsed draws, or 50 if none; errors and limits stop and resume).
- **AUC:** pair 3 abusive against pair 3 legitimate, pooled over the two *s* = 0.75 worlds, on the
  mean of the 3 draws. Bootstrap: 2,000 resamples of units within (world, class), seed 20261093,
  shared by Haiku and F64 (paired).
- **F64:** held-out logistic and trees, fitted by `r0.score_pool` on all units of the two
  *s* = 0.75 worlds pooled (pairs 4–6 train, pair 3 tests, as G2), scored on the same 160 units.
- **CONFIRMED if all three hold**, at the 2.5th / 97.5th percentiles (one test; no Bonferroni):
  - (a) few-shot AUC, lower bound > 0.5;
  - (b) few-shot − F64 trees and few-shot − F64 logistic, both lower bounds > 0;
  - (c) the *s* = 0 control: the few-shot AUC's 95% interval contains 0.5 (40 + 40 units, its own
    within-class resamples, same seed). If (c) fails, the verdict is **LEAK**, whatever (a) and (b)
    say.
- Otherwise **NOT CONFIRMED**. No rescue rule, no prompt change and no re-draw after a number is
  seen.

## Known risks (stated before results)

- **Winner's curse.** 0.84 was the best of eight cells; expect less. Planning arithmetic
  (Hanley–McNeil, 80 + 80, independent errors, F64 at 0.55): power for (b) is about 0.92 at a true
  few-shot AUC of 0.75, 0.69 at 0.70, and 0.36 at 0.65. A true effect near 0.65 will usually read
  NOT CONFIRMED. (b) binds before (a).
- **The control is small.** 40 + 40 units: it false-flags LEAK about 5% of the time under a true
  null, and it catches only a large artifact.
- **Few-shot compression.** M8's few-shot answered in a narrow high band; ties pull its AUC toward
  0.5. The prompt stays frozen.
- Claude Code's environment block (now including the date 2026-10-30 and CLI 2.1.314) differs from
  M8's. It is constant within M9.
