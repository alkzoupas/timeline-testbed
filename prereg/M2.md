# Pre-registration: M2, R0 across the sweep, gate G1 (2026-10-21)

Written by the coordinator before any R0 score was computed and before the new sweep worlds' health
output was read. SPEC §7.1 (G1), §5.6, §6.3, §6.4. Generator `tt-gen-v1` (DECISIONS).

## Before the experiment (AGENTS.md)

1. **Decision it changes.** Pass: the testbed is valid; M3 and M4 run on generator v1 and these worlds.
   Fail: fix the generator (SPEC §7.1, at most twice; each fix voids earlier worlds and needs the owner
   and a DECISIONS line), then stop and report to the owner.
2. **Independent groups per class.** Per *s*, two worlds pooled: 480 abusive / 1,920 legitimate; the
   four T families 320 / 1,280; each held-out test side's T families 160 / 640; each family 80 / 320.
3. **Full-size smoke test.** The R0 route runs end to end on the pooled *s* = 1 worlds (2,400 groups)
   before *s* = 0 is scored; recorded in STATUS.md.

## Worlds

Standard scale, *s* ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}, seeds 1 and 2 (`runs/m1-dev-*`, `runs/m2-dev-*`), plus the
channel world (seed 101) and the generator-only sample (seed 201). No world is replaced or re-drawn
after its result is seen.

## G1 passes only if all four hold

- **(a) Health.** `health world` passes on all ten sweep worlds (one Bonferroni family per world,
  DECISIONS); the generator-only sample passes.
- **(b) Positive control.** On the two *s* = 1 worlds pooled, the oracle AUC of each family against its
  twin is ≥ 0.90 (six items, point estimates).
- **(c) R0 null rule at *s* = 0** (SPEC §5.6 rule as implemented: two-sided permutation test of
  |AUC − 0.5|), on the two *s* = 0 worlds pooled, as **one Bonferroni family of T = 62** items at
  0.05 / 62:
  - random and detector (no fitting, so one score per unit): each family against its twin (6), and
    all abusive against all legitimate (1): 2 × 7 = 14;
  - F12 logistic, F64-w90 logistic, F64-w90 trees, in each regime (in-family 5-fold CV; held-out
    folds A and B, every unit scored by the fold that tests it): 3 × 2 × 7 = 42;
  - oracle: each family against its twin: 6.
- **(d) T tags.** `health channel` (channel world plus the two standard *s* = 1 worlds) passes, and its
  measured tags equal `MEASURED_TAGS` for all six pairs.

## Known false-failure risk (stated before results)

(c) fails a fair generator at most 5% of the time. Each world's health family failed 2/100 fair
seeds (nuisance plus negative control; M1). The five new worlds therefore carry roughly a 10% chance
of at least one chance failure. No rescue rule: a failure is reported as a G1 failure, with the item
and its p, and the owner decides.

## Reported, not gating

Per *s* and regime: the primary metric (SPEC §6.4: mean over the two test sides of the AUC of the
side's T abusive groups against their own twins), per-family, per-channel and pooled AUC, recall at
FPR 5% and 10%, 95% group-bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples, within each test side and class), and
paired-bootstrap differences (F64 trees − F64 logistic; stronger F64 − detector). The oracle bound
for a pooled comparison is the mean of its per-family oracle AUCs.
