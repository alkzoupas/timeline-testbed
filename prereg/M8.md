## Scoring (once, `python -m testbed.eval.haiku score`)

- **Draws.** A draw whose answer doesn't parse is retried up to twice (3 attempts). A unit's score
  per variant is the mean of its parsed draws, or 50 if none parsed. Limit and API errors, and
  responses with no output tokens, aren't draws: the run stops and resumes. So do 3 unparsed
  attempts in a row (they stay and count). The pilot is scored only when every draw is done.
- **Primary** (SPEC §6.4 on the pilot units): per test side, the AUC of its T abusive units against
  the twins of the same pairs; the mean of sides A and B. Paired bootstrap: R0's 2,000 resamples
  within (side, class), seed 20261033, shared by both variants and F64.
- **Go-bar: GO if either variant's primary lower bound is above 0.5**, the bound being the 1.25th
  percentile of the resamples (a two-sided 97.5% interval: SPEC's 95%, Bonferroni-adjusted for the
  two variants, as G2 did). Otherwise NO-GO. No rescue rule, no prompt change and no re-draw after a
  pilot number is seen.

## Known risks (stated before results)

- Power, per variant: at a draw reliability of 0.2 and true AUC 0.65, about 0.8 at this bound (0.88
  at the 2.5th, `reports/M8.md`); at 0.75 near 1. A true AUC near 0.55 will usually read NO-GO.
- Few-shot may compress: in the check it answered 93–96 on 11 of 12 draws, both classes. Ties pull
  its AUC toward 0.5. Four units, so stated, not acted on: the prompt stays frozen.
- No *s* = 0 control in the pilot: a GO could come from a render artifact that doesn't depend on
  *s*. The full run carries the control (`m1-dev-s0-seed1` and `seed2`).
- `m1-dev-s0.75-seed1` is a G2 gate world, already scored for F64 and R2. Nothing in the prompt
  or the examples was tuned on it, but it can't confirm a GO.
- Claude Code's environment block (cwd, date, platform, model) and account note are constant
  context. The call order is seeded and interleaved, so drift can't line up with class.
- Usage limits: about 1,600 output tokens per call (thinking on), so about 3.1 M for the pilot. A
  limit stops the driver; it resumes later, and the owner hears how far it got.

## Confirmation rule

A GO is a pilot finding, not a claim. The claim needs a new pre-registration and worlds the pilot
didn't touch: `m2-dev-s0.75-seed2` plus fresh seeds, with the *s* = 0 control. `runs/confirm-*`
stays closed (M6's).

## Reported, not gating

Draw-1 primary alone; retest reliability across the 3 draws (pairwise Pearson and Spearman,
Spearman–Brown at *k* = 3); per-pair AUCs; the parse rate; Haiku − F64 logistic and trees on the
same resamples (F64 recomputed on G2's pool and checked against M2); call times and tokens.
